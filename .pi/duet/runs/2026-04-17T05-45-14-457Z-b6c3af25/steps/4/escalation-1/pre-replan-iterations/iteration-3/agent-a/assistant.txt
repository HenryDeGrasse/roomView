Fixed one concrete auth/flow inconsistency in `docs/mvp_duet.md`.

What I changed:
- Added `video_upload_token` to the `POST /captures/roomplan` response.
- Updated the capture flow so optional raw video upload uses that token.
- Changed `POST /captures/{scene_id}/video` to require the server-issued `video_upload_token` instead of an authenticated scene-scoped session.

Why:
- The previous spec made optional video upload happen immediately after scene creation, before the user redeems the web handoff into a scene-scoped session.
- Requiring authenticated scene access for that mobile upload path was therefore inconsistent and not actually usable.
